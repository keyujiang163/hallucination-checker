Frequently asked

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If Pro or Team isn't a fit within 14 days of your first charge, email us and we'll refund the full amount — no forms, no friction. You can also cancel any time from your dashboard and you'll keep access through the end of the current billing period.

How do you handle data and privacy?

We never train on your inputs. Each check is processed in real time, the prompt and evidence are discarded after the report is returned, and full request logs are opt-in. Billing is handled by Stripe — we never see your card number — and the open-source core means you can audit exactly what's running on our servers.

Can I add custom search sources?

Pro plans can define domain whitelists and trusted feeds (PubMed, SEC filings, internal wikis via webhook). Team plans unlock per-tenant knowledge bases — drop in PDFs or URLs and we'll embed them into the evidence pool. Custom enterprise connectors are available on request.
